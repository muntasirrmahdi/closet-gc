Questions You Might Have

What is Closet GC?

Closet GC is a free tool that shows you which clothes you actually wear. Just like your phone has a trash bin for old files, this tool finds clothes in your closet that you never use. You add your items, tap when you wear them, and the tool shows what's been sitting there for months. No download. No account. Just open it and start.

Is it really free?

Yes, totally free. No hidden costs. No ads. No "premium" version. Your data stays on your device -- we have nothing to sell you.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. It stays on your device inside your browser. We can't see your clothes, your notes, or anything you add. But this also means: if you clear your browser data, your closet goes with it. Use the Export button to save a backup file.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. Tap Export to save your closet as a file. Email it to yourself, keep it in Google Drive, or use it to move your data to another device. Tap Import to bring it back.

What counts as unused?

An item is "unused" when you haven't worn it in a while. The default is 90 days -- about 3 months. You can change this from 7 days up to a full year. Brand new items get time before we start counting.

Do I need to sign up?

No. No email. No password. No account. Open the page and start. Just remember: if you lose your data, we can't get it back. That's why we keep reminding you to export.

Does this use AI or send my data anywhere?

No AI. No servers. No internet needed after the first load. It's all simple math -- the tool checks the date of your last wear and decides if it's been too long. Your data stays private because there's nowhere for it to go. You can even use it without internet.
